# Dashboard KPI Definitions

## Total Cases
Count of unique patient case IDs.

## Total Events
Count of recorded patient activities.

## Average Case Duration
Average time between the first and last
recorded event for each patient case.

## Loop-back Rate
Percentage of cases containing a specified
repeated activity pattern.

## Conformance Percentage
Percentage of cases following the defined
ideal clinical pathway.

## Before vs After
Compare waiting time before and after the
simulated intake-form fix.

Note: Calculate results from validated data.
Do not assume final results in advance.
